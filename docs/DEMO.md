# Demo Script (3 min)

1. Open frontend (<http://localhost:5173>) and backend (<http://localhost:8000>).
2. In Chat, type: "Factura a Juan por 5 horas de consultoria a $50/hora, IVA 18%".
3. Click Extract -> review JSON.
4. Click Create Invoice -> invoice created.
5. Click PDF link in Dashboard to view the generated PDF (with payment link).
6. (Optional) Click Send to PayPal to create a PayPal invoice (requires sandbox creds).
7. Dashboard shows total billed/pending/paid and invoice status.
8. Webhook (PayPal) updates status to paid when payment completes.

Notes: video must have no third-party trademarks or copyrighted music.
